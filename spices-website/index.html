    01 / 07 · The seed

    One capsule, a forest crop.

    Green cardamom is the fruit of Elettaria cardamomum, a perennial of the ginger family native to the evergreen forests of the Western Ghats. Each capsule holds 15–20 aromatic seeds, raised in shaded nurseries before planting out.

    Botanical name
    Elettaria cardamomum
    Family
    Zingiberaceae
    Propagation
    Seedlings or rhizome divisions
    02 / 07 · Planting

    Planted in the shade.

    Seedlings go out with the monsoon, under the canopy of tall forest trees. Cardamom needs filtered light, rich forest soil and steady humidity all year.

    Rainfall
    1,500–4,000 mm / year
    Altitude
    600–1,500 m
    Shade
    40–60 % filtered light
    03 / 07 · The plant

    A clump of leafy shoots.

    From an underground rhizome the plant sends up tall leafy shoots with long, glossy, lance-shaped leaves. A healthy clump bears its first crop in the second or third year.

    Shoot height
    2–4 m
    First harvest
    Year 2–3
    Productive life
    10–15 years
    04 / 07 · Flowering

    Flowers at ground level.

    Cardamom flowers on long panicles that grow from the base of the plant and creep across the forest floor. Small white flowers streaked with violet give way to plump green capsules.

    Panicles
    From the base, along the ground
    Flower
    White, violet-streaked lip
    Capsule maturity
    ≈ 120 days after flowering
    05 / 07 · Harvest

    Picked green, pod by pod.

    Capsules are picked by hand when fully mature but still green, in rounds through the season. Picking at the right moment keeps both the colour and the aromatic oil.

    Picking rounds
    Every 15–30 days
    Method
    Hand-picked
    Stage
    Mature, still green
    06 / 07 · Curing

    Cured to keep the green.

    Fresh capsules are cured on trays in a heated curing house rather than in the sun. Gentle, even heat draws out moisture while holding the green colour buyers pay for.

    Curing temp.
    45–50 °C
    Curing time
    ≈ 18–36 hours
    Target moisture
    ≤ 10 %
    07 / 07 · Pack & ship

    Polished, graded, packed, sealed.

    Cured capsules are rubbed clean of stalks, graded by size and colour, tested and packed to your specification. We stuff and seal the container at origin and prepare the export documents.

    Grading
    7–8 mm, deep green
    Packing
    10 / 25 kg lined cartons or bags
