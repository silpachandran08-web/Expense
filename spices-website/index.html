01 · The seed

It begins with one peppercorn

Black pepper (Piper nigrum) starts as a seed or a cutting from a healthy mother vine, planted in rich, well-drained tropical soil.

🌱 Healthy planting stock is where quality starts.
02 · Sprouting

The monsoon wakes it up

Warm rain and shade coax out the first shoot and heart-shaped leaves. Farmers plant it beside a living support tree or pole.

🌧️ Pepper loves humidity, heavy rainfall and partial shade.
03 · The climbing vine

A vine that reaches for the sky

Pepper is a climber. Over three years the vine spirals up its support, gripping it with aerial roots, before it bears its first crop.

🌿 A healthy vine keeps producing for 20+ years.
04 · Flowering & berries

Spikes of green pearls

Tiny flowers form on hanging spikes, each carrying dozens of berries. Over six to eight months they swell and ripen from green to red.

🫛 One spike holds 50–150 berries.
05 · Hand harvest

Plucked at the perfect moment

When the first berries on a spike blush red, the whole spike is hand-picked and gathered in baskets, then threshed to free the berries.

🧺 Timing the harvest decides pungency and colour.
06 · Sun drying

The sun turns green to black

Berries are spread on clean mats and sun-dried for 7–10 days. They shrink, wrinkle and turn the deep black every kitchen knows.

☀️ Dried to safe moisture levels for long storage.
07 · Grade, pack & export

Graded, packed, shipped

We clean, sort and grade by size and density, then pack in jute or PP bags to your specification and load full or part containers.

🚢 Export documentation handled end to end.
